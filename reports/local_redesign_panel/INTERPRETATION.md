# Interpretation and reserve candidate

The most useful reserve is **`apical_short_008_A41H_mpnn03`**, which adds A34K/A38R
to A41H. It passes 8/10 raw samples, both selected raw poses, and both strict
preparation checks. The two pKa shifts are −1.042/−1.046, and the His-only release
proxies are 4.477/4.451. Its free pKas are 7.207/7.117, outside the frozen 6–7
band. The matched baseline and original parent also do not qualify under all
strict preparation checks, and the full prepared control panel fails. Consequently
this is an unqualified reserve, not an admitted replacement or proven improvement.
The pKa band is a heuristic; being slightly outside it is not experimental evidence
of failure. Conversely, a larger computational ratio is not evidence of stronger
binding or measured pH release.

The descriptive PROPKA decomposition attributes −1.015/−1.045 of the shift to its
volume-desolvation term, with net Coulomb contributions of only −0.027/−0.0002.
These are offsets in pKa units, not binding energies. Within this model, the signal
is predominantly burial-associated; it does not demonstrate a new histidine–target
cation repulsion mechanism. This decomposition reproduces the same predictions
and is not independent validation.

The A41H baseline's seed-2909 raw clash is target Tyr211 OH against binder Asp43
OD2 at 1.924 Å. A38K and A34E/A38E also have short contacts at this pair in that
seed (1.773/1.792 Å). The successful A34K/A38R redesign avoids the frozen clash
screen in both selected poses. This is useful geometry evidence, with only two
selected poses and no experimental binding measurement.

The positive control's new strict movement failure is at the cropped receptor's
N terminus, more than 21 Å from the binder; it passes the separate interface
sensitivity check. The scrambled control fails that sensitivity check. Its
preparation instability does not erase the raw positive/negative confidence
separation, but the predeclared all-controls preparation condition remains unmet.
