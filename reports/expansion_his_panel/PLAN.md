# Bounded histidine follow-up on three new parents

This plan is recorded before new predictions. It tests 24 new sequences: five singles and three pairs for each of expansion_apical_long_092, expansion_apical_short_018 and expansion_apical_short_026. The three unchanged parents, prior apical_short_008 parent and A41H comparison, and three structural controls produce 32 cases. Seed 1709 with five diffusion samples gives 160 predictions.

Sites must satisfy contact and free/buried-SASA bounds in both previously selected parent poses. Possible opposite-charge contacts below 3.5 Å and residues in the overlapping top 20% of contact-pair count and buried SASA in either pose are excluded as anchor proxies. These are geometric heuristics, not energetic measurements. Up to three peripheral and two partially buried sites are preferred; long_092 has no eligible partially buried site and uses five peripheral sites. Pair Cα distances must be 5–16 Å in both poses. Full site metrics, excluded positions and source hashes are retained in `data/expansion_his_panel/contact_audit.json`.

Some selected sites lie near native binder termini (positions 3/4 or 60). The actual His conformation and proximity to termini must be reviewed if they advance. These models do not include the assay tag; a favorable pKa result will not establish tag compatibility. No rule assumes that proximity to Arg alone proves release direction.

Selection uses the unchanged raw confidence/pose/contact/context/clash checks and the frozen target-integrity filter. Every sample remains recorded. If all selected controls pass, at most two passing new variants per parent advance by selected interface ipTM, with lexical tie breaks. All three parents and both prior comparison sequences remain in confirmation, even if they fail the first screen. Confirmation uses seeds 1801 and 1901, five samples per seed, at most 140 predictions. A failed selected model is never replaced by an unselected passing model.

The same local preparation is applied once to every selected candidate and control, with failures retained. Preparation never changes a raw pass/fail decision. PROPKA is run only on variants passing both selected fresh-seed raw checks, with controls passing and prepared poses satisfying the original geometry and movement checks. Introduced histidines are already present in the independently predicted structures. The strict direction thresholds are unchanged: every bound-minus-free shift ≤−0.5, at least one free pKa in 6–7, the independent-His Kd(6.0)/Kd(7.4) proxy ≥2, and distance to artificial receptor termini ≥10 Å. Report matched-parent qualification separately; a variant-only pass does not establish increased affinity. A41H is an earlier computational comparison, not an experimentally validated pH-positive control.

Two $2 reservations cover the screen and confirmation within the existing $30 gross project cap. One L40S container per phase, no retries, a 2,100-second work limit, a 2,250-second function limit and a 120-second startup bound constrain cost. The bound including resource maxima and a $0.40 miscellaneous allowance is below $2 per phase at the checked [Modal rates](https://modal.com/pricing). The launcher conservatively carries the entire preceding round's $12 allocation against its earlier baseline, despite much lower reported usage. No further generation, mouse predictions, upstream issue/PR or challenge submission is part of this round.
