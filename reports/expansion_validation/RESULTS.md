Higher ipTM than the positive control is not evidence of higher affinity. The other new parents pass 6/10, 8/10 and 8/10 samples; these rates are reported rather than hidden behind the selected-model rule.

All selected models, including failures and controls, undergo the same frozen preparation diagnostic. Raw gates remain unchanged. No histidine redesign or pH-switch claim is made for these new parents in this experiment. The four passes, plus the existing A41H hypothesis and its parent, enter a separate advisory human/mouse target-conformation comparison; that comparison is not a calibrated mouse-binding classifier.

This confirmation is bounded at $1.50 from the existing validation budget within the $30 total. Every input, prediction sample, target-integrity rejection and selected representative is retained. No upstream issue, PR or challenge submission was filed.
