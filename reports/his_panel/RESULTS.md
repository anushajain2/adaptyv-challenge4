# Expanded histidine screen: six variants retain the interface in the selected model

All 125 predictions completed: 25 sequences × five samples, seed 1009. All three selected controls pass. Both unmodified parents and six of the 20 mutant/comparator sequences pass the unchanged selected-model screen. These are one-seed computational screens, not qualified binders or evidence of pH switching.

The positive control scores interface ipTM 0.646; the scrambled and unrelated-VHH presumed negatives score 0.140 and 0.130. The target-integrity filter excludes four of 125 samples. Every original sample is retained.

## Diagnostic substitutions

D51H alone fails the full screen in all five samples, with interface ipTM 0.088–0.116. Its selected representative loses the intended interface. One unselected low-confidence sample retains 58.3% of the reference backbone contacts, so it would be inaccurate to say every D51H sample has zero contacts. The double-mutant comparator also fails all five, ipTM 0.098–0.114, with contact recall 0–5.6%.

T70H alone retains 69.4–80.6% of reference contacts and fails only the confidence criterion in its selected representative: all five interface ipTM scores are 0.492–0.496, below 0.50. This strengthens the hypothesis that changing Asp51 is particularly disruptive in this predictor, but does not prove the salt bridge is the causal energetic anchor. Both substitutions and their interactions can affect predictions.
