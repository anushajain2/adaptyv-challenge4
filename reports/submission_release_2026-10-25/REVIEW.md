## What the audit found

All eight cores lack tryptophan and have acidic sequence-estimated pIs. The original six have no cysteines; new short_055 has two. New long_037 has mildly positive GRAVY (+0.155), while the other cores have negative mean hydropathy. These descriptors do not demonstrate soluble expression, folding or stability.

- **diversity_short_055 models a C31–C42 disulfide.** The raw S–S distances are 2.003/1.870 Å; preparation infers an oxidized bond and gives 2.039/2.036 Å. Correct bond formation is unmeasured. Adaptyv [documents a disulfide-supporting cell-free system](https://docs.adaptyvbio.com/wiki/technologies/cell-free-protein-expression) with DsbC and glutathione, which supports platform feasibility but does not validate this particular bond. See the [disulfide audit](https://github.com/anushajain2/adaptyv-challenge4/blob/8cda1bad59ee6d15af46071b1545e69d7a628a3d/reports/diversity_confirmation/disulfide_audit.json).
- **diversity_long_037 has an NTS sequon at 24–26.** It is a context-dependent motif flag, not evidence of glycosylation in the assay. Neither new parent contains histidine; their pH behavior is unestablished.
- **short_026 has an NNT sequon at residues 26–28** and NT motifs at 27 and 31. The sequon is a context-dependent glycosylation review flag, not evidence that this assay construct is glycosylated. It is retained unchanged.
- **long_092 has an AAAA run at 73–76**, with DG motifs at 9 and 45. These are compositional/chemical review flags, not validated degradation or aggregation predictions.
- **short_026 and short_015 contain methionine**, at 46 and 13 respectively. Oxidation susceptibility requires structural and experimental context.
- A41H, its parent and long_092 have calculated bare-core aromatic/disulfide A280 extinction coefficients of zero. This is a concentration-measurement caveat for bare proteins, not a binding failure; the actual tagged assay construct differs.

The complete positions, composition, pI, charge, hydropathy and provenance are in [audit.json](https://github.com/anushajain2/adaptyv-challenge4/blob/8cda1bad59ee6d15af46071b1545e69d7a628a3d/reports/submission_draft/audit.json). [Biopython ProtParam](https://biopython.org/docs/latest/api/Bio.SeqUtils.ProtParam.html) supplies the sequence descriptors; the pI calculation uses a 0–14 search interval to avoid clipping an acidic sequence at the default lower bound. Charges and pIs here are sequence estimates, separate from structure-dependent PROPKA and the pH-release mechanism. There is no calibrated developability score or claim of acceptable expression, immunogenicity, aggregation or shelf stability.

Across different parent lineages, global aligned sequence identity is **14.7–33.9%**. A41H and its parent are **98.3% identical** and count as one lineage. The original sequence-aligned backbone comparisons and angle compositions are descriptive only and do not establish seven different fold classes. All candidates come from the same BoltzGen apical campaign.

The subsequent [database similarity review](https://github.com/anushajain2/adaptyv-challenge4/blob/8cda1bad59ee6d15af46071b1545e69d7a628a3d/reports/novelty_review/RESULTS.md) adds current RCSB sequence searches, Foldseek searches against PDB100 and AlphaFold/Swiss-Prot, local TM-align, and completed local Swiss-Prot BLASTp against release 2026_03 (575,748 canonical sequences). The human P02786 retrieval control passes exactly; no candidate meets the frozen close-sequence threshold, although weak local matches are reported. The original remote BLAST job was left pending at that review and is not the source of the completed evidence. Masked segments and canonical-only database coverage limit sensitivity. The key caveat is **short_015's close backbone match to AF1782 (2OO2)** in both poses: all 61 design residues align, with TM-align RMSDs 1.450/1.467 Å and 27.6% global sequence identity. It remains a known-backbone binding backup, not a novel-fold claim. Other candidates also have partial or moderate structural matches. Database versions, short-sequence sensitivity and incomplete known-protein coverage prevent a global novelty verdict. That original review left its six sequences and rank order unchanged; its exact CSV/FASTA are now archived alongside it. The later diversity round adds two sequences without editing the original six.

The [two-parent database review](https://github.com/anushajain2/adaptyv-challenge4/blob/8cda1bad59ee6d15af46071b1545e69d7a628a3d/reports/diversity_novelty/RESULTS.md) applies the same methods to both confirmed poses of each added design. ## Final experiment and submission decision

The [final twelve-variant His panel](https://github.com/anushajain2/adaptyv-challenge4/blob/8cda1bad59ee6d15af46071b1545e69d7a628a3d/reports/final_his_panel/RESULTS.md) completed 160 predictions. N40H, S90H, T74H and S72H passed both selected raw structural checks, but none met the full two-pose direction criteria. S90H had modest His-only ratio proxies of 1.71 and 1.87; S72H passed the direction heuristic in one pose but its other pose failed preparation movement. A prepared positive control also failed the 1.0 Å maximum-backbone-movement guard at 1.017 Å, so the prepared control panel does not qualify. These are computational thresholds, not proof that the variants cannot bind or switch experimentally.

All twelve variants remain outside the CSV. The conditional tag run was skipped by the frozen rule. The unchanged long_037 parent passed both newly selected raw poses (7/10 samples), and its latest two models and per-round evidence are now included. The eight sequences and their ordering are unchanged.
