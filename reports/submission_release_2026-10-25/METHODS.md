# Methods accompanying the draft

## Scope and provenance

These are computational hypotheses for human TfR1 binding and, for A41H, an acidic-release direction. No binding, pH-selectivity, mouse-cross-reactivity or expression measurements are available. The eight ranked sequences represent seven generated parent lineages; A41H is a substitution in our own generated short_008 parent. Published binders were calibration controls, not submitted sequences or starting binder templates.

The retained designs originate from corrected BoltzGen apical campaigns against the bound 6WRW receptor crop, native residues 190–383. Native/crop mapping is explicit. Full-dimer and observed-glycan context is restored for steric auditing while retaining the modeled bound crop. Observed glycans are not a complete glycan ensemble. The crop omits native position 142; its genotype correction is handled in full-target preparation, not an invented residue within the crop.

BoltzGen revision `247b9bbd8b68a60aba854c2968d6a0cddd21ad6d` and Adaptyv-derived Modal wrappers underpin generation/refolding. Binding and exclusion labels are separate parser entries, and actual feature tokens are checked. The earliest 75-design pilot had zero positive hotspot labels and is not evidence for successful hotspot conditioning. Corrected miniprotein generation uses native hotspots 208, 210 and 211. Conserved-patch and nanobody branches did not yield retained parents. See [generation methods](https://github.com/anushajain2/adaptyv-challenge4/blob/8cda1bad59ee6d15af46071b1545e69d7a628a3d/reports/next_round/METHODS.md) and [campaign results](https://github.com/anushajain2/adaptyv-challenge4/blob/8cda1bad59ee6d15af46071b1545e69d7a628a3d/reports/RESULTS.md).

## Structural evidence

Refolding templates contain the receptor only; binder coordinates are excluded. This campaign uses a single-sequence/no-MSA configuration supported by its own limited control comparison, not a general claim that MSAs harm prediction. The published 2DS25.5 complex is the structural positive. Scrambled sequence and unrelated VHH controls are presumed negatives, not experimentally established nonbinders. Chai-1 did not provide independent interface confirmation.

Each round defines model selection before geometry inspection. Historical confirmation uses the frozen confidence-based selection after a target-integrity check. The latest bare/tagged comparison selects the minimum mean target/core cross-PAE among target-integrity-eligible samples; core PAE replaces whole-chain ipTM to avoid suffix dilution. These rules are reported separately, without pooling their pass rates into one success estimate.

Structural screens cover binder fold, receptor-frame interface pose, contacts to the generated design reference, intended hotspots, context fit and counted clashes. Reference-contact recall for a novel design is self-consistency, not experimentally observed interface recovery. Selected failed poses remain failures; favorable alternative samples do not replace them.

The final bounded diversity round generated 100 designs (60 short, 40 long), yielding 99 unique sequences and no exact match to the 495 earlier designs. Five passed the initial single-sample screen and the prospectively frozen sequence/backbone redundancy checks. A fixed two-seed, five-sample confirmation retained two parents: long_037 (8/10 samples) and short_055 (9/10); all six selected control checks passed. All 16 selected models, including failures and controls, underwent the same v2 preparation. Both retained parents passed movement and internal-geometry guards in both poses. Raw clash failures in the other three candidates were not rescued. Their [complete round evidence](https://github.com/anushajain2/adaptyv-challenge4/blob/8cda1bad59ee6d15af46071b1545e69d7a628a3d/reports/diversity_confirmation/RESULTS.md) is archived.

The final bounded His experiment used ten neutral peripheral singles and two pairs on long_037, preserving charged residues, major contact proxies and five terminal residues at each end in both source poses. All twelve variants, the parent and three controls were predicted with seeds 2503/2609 and five samples each (160 total). Four variants passed both selected raw checks; the parent passed both with 7/10 raw sample passes. All 32 selected models, including failures and controls, received the same v2 preparation. No variant qualified in both poses for direction, and the prepared positive-control panel also failed a movement guard. The conditional tag experiment was skipped. All twelve variants are excluded, and the original eight submission sequences and ordering are unchanged. The [complete final experiment](https://github.com/anushajain2/adaptyv-challenge4/blob/8cda1bad59ee6d15af46071b1545e69d7a628a3d/reports/final_his_panel/RESULTS.md) preserves individual exclusions and modest shifts without promoting them to measured release.

## Direction and construct context

Histidine variants are independently predicted before pKa evaluation. Free and bound PROPKA calculations use the same binder conformation, and the introduced-His-only proton-linkage ratio is a conditional mechanism proxy rather than an affinity prediction. The frozen heuristics require shifts at most −0.5, a free His pKa within 6–7, ratio proxy at least 2, and separation from artificial receptor termini. Preparation warnings and movement failures exclude interpretation; minimization never rescues a raw structural failure.

The original preparation fixed receptor coordinates. A separately frozen v2 protocol restrained both backbones while allowing receptor sidechains and hydrogen coordinates to move, applying the same procedure to parents, variants and controls. Revised calculations on old predictions are sensitivity analyses. Some control/movement checks still fail, and zero qualified two-pose pH switches were obtained. All of these exclusions are preserved.

A matched bare/tagged panel tests the published 64-residue platform-default linker/split-GFP11/Twin-Strep suffix as an explicit challenge-construct assumption. Core scoring excludes suffix contacts; full-construct pKa retains the suffix where modeled. Flexible suffix placements have low confidence, so tag-independence failures cannot be read as demonstrated experimental interference. Bare sequences alone appear in the draft upload. The two newly added parents have no modeled assay suffix and no histidines. Short_055 has C31/C42 coordinates consistent with a disulfide in both raw poses; OpenMM infers this bond and preserves it during preparation. This explicitly assumes an oxidized conformation, without experimental confirmation of bond formation. The platform documents support for disulfide-containing proteins, but that does not establish correct folding of this sequence.

## Ranking, diversity and sequence review

Ranking prioritizes the repeated A41H direction hypothesis, then repeatedly retained parent interfaces, additional lineages and a matched parent comparator. It is a transparent editorial choice rather than a trained scoring function. All candidates have unresolved mouse cross-reactivity; the matched human-AlphaFold positive failed in the mouse exploration, preventing an interpretable mouse classification.

Sequence descriptors use installed Biopython, whose version is recorded in the audit. Motif scans and composition flags are descriptive only and did not change any sequence. Global alignment uses match 2, mismatch −1, gap-open −4 and gap-extension −0.5, with identity divided by the longer sequence. The initial descriptive backbone RMSD uses sequence-matched Cα pairs. It is distinct from receptor-fitted interface RMSD used in the structural screen. The first selected model in each latest bare round is used even when it fails a screen.

The [database review](https://github.com/anushajain2/adaptyv-challenge4/blob/8cda1bad59ee6d15af46071b1545e69d7a628a3d/reports/novelty_review/RESULTS.md) searches both selected bare poses per design with Foldseek against the server's PDB100 20240101 and AlphaFold/Swiss-Prot v6 collections, then runs local TM-align (tmtools 0.3.0) on the first ten server-ranked hits per query/database. Receptor and tag atoms are excluded. Both length-normalized scores and coverage are recorded; selected matches are verified against original PDB coordinates. Current RCSB experimental-sequence searches are reported with retrieval controls. After the remote NCBI BLAST job remained WAITING for over an hour, a prospective local fallback preserved its seven queries and screening rules. BLAST+ 2.17.0 searched the checksum-verified UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot canonical release 2026_03 (575,748 sequences; 209,017,843 residues), with BLOSUM62, word size 3, E-value limit 10, SEG soft masking, composition-based statistics mode 2 and at most 100 target sequences. The separate isoform supplement was excluded. Human P02786 was recovered exactly across the 194-residue query; zero candidate HSPs meet identity ≥50%, query coverage ≥80% and E ≤0.001. Weak local matches and raw masking intervals are archived. The rule is per HSP, not a union of disjoint alignments; masking and short-sequence composition limit sensitivity. The remote RID was left pending at the original review and is not represented as complete. These are limited database comparisons, not exhaustive novelty proofs. Short_015's AF1782/2OO2 similarity is disclosed; sharing a known backbone does not demonstrate sequence copying or a shared binding interface. No sequence or ranking was changed to optimize these search results.

The [additional two-parent review](https://github.com/anushajain2/adaptyv-challenge4/blob/8cda1bad59ee6d15af46071b1545e69d7a628a3d/reports/diversity_novelty/RESULTS.md) uses the same versions, databases and thresholds, with both selected poses per added parent and matching retrieval controls. All searches completed; no close-sequence flags arose, and followed-up structural matches were weak or partial. An exact public-byte check preceded API uploads. The local Swiss-Prot database was reused; no new remote NCBI job was submitted. The initial six-sequence review remains a separate historical record.
